CoherentHand: Temporally Consistent 3D Hand Trajectory Synthesis with Semantic Motion Priors

Bikram Boote1,  Junho Kim1,  Ozgur Kara1,  Sangmin Lee2,  James M. Rehg1

1University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign  ·  2Korea University

Abstract

Understanding 3D hand motion from visual and linguistic cues is crucial for modeling Human-Object Interaction (HOI). Current state-of-the-art 3D HOI generation frameworks suffer from two key limitations: 1) Methods for learning hand-motion priors fail to capture subtle, object-specific finger articulation; and 2) Discretized motion generators produce jerky, physically implausible motion trajectories. We introduce CoherentHand, a unified and object-model-free framework that addresses these critical limitations to achieve robust and fluid 3D hand trajectory estimation.

Our approach introduces two key innovations: First, we propose a VLM-guided Residual Vector-Quantized (RVQ) codebook that leverages intermediate Vision-Language Model (VLM) representations to infuse high-level affordance and articulation cues directly into the motion priors, ensuring fine-grained semantic alignment with the visual context. Second, we employ a flow-based decoder conditioned on this enriched codebook to generate continuous 3D hand pose sequences, thereby overcoming the temporal noise artifacts inherent in discrete token prediction. Evaluated across challenging forecasting and interpolation tasks on the HoloAssist and ARCTIC datasets, CoherentHand establishes new state-of-the-art performance across four generalization settings, demonstrating high fidelity in terms of finger articulation and trajectory fluency.

Key Contributions

  • Unified Framework: Highly precise and temporally coherent 3D hand interaction trajectory estimation conditioned on a single image and action prompt without requiring explicit 3D object models at inference.
  • VLM-Guided RVQ Codebook: Uses intermediate VLM representations to infuse high-level affordance and articulation cues during detokenization, achieving fine-grained semantic alignment.
  • Flow-Based Trajectory Generator: Decodes discrete tokens into continuous 3D hand pose sequences, effectively overcoming the frame-level noise artifacts inherent in discrete token-prediction models.
  • State-of-the-Art Performance: Achieves superior fidelity and fluency on the rigorous HoloAssist and ARCTIC benchmarks across multiple structural generalization settings.

Method Overview

The architecture operates in two complementary stages that work in synergy to achieve object-conditioned, temporally smooth motion generation:

Figure 1: Hand-Object Interaction Synthesis. Top row: Standard VQ representations with Transformer generators rely on generic motion priors, leading to coarse and inconsistent trajectories. Bottom row: CoherentHand introduces VLM-guided tokenization and a flow-based generator for fine-grained and smooth 3D hand interactions.
Figure 2: Method Pipeline Architecture. (a) Stage 1: Learning a VLM-guided RVQ codebook by reconstructing 3D hand interaction trajectories using high-level semantics from a large Vision-Language Model. (b) Stage 2: A flow-based decoder is conditioned on the codebook and other multi-modal inputs (image, text, and contact indicators) to generate fluid final trajectories.
